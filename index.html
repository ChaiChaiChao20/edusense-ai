Signals without surveillance.

Face landmarks run in the browser. The app does not identify students, store frames, record video, or send class footage to a server. Aggregate summaries are exported only when you choose. MediaPipe's library and model are fetched from their public hosts; the library may send usage metrics as described in its privacy notice.
